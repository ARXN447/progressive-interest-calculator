How it works

Daily-rest interest is generally based on the outstanding amount already disbursed by the bank, the annual rate and the number of days. As more loan funds are disbursed, the interest usually increases.

Important: This is an estimate, not a bank quotation. Construction progress does not always equal the percentage of the loan disbursed. For a more accurate estimate, enter the actual amount disbursed and calculate each period separately if the balance or rate changed during the month. Your bank may use its own day-count convention and terms.
